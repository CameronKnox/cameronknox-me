## The $216 Packing Cubes

So recently I spent way too much money on some Zpacks packing cubes. Two of the large ones were $100, two of the mediums were $80, and one of the small ones was $34. All in, I spent about $216 on packing cubes.

Some people might think, why the hell would you ever do that? And I get it. But I really like the light material they use, which is Dyneema. It's what a lot of tents are made out of, so if you're a big backpacker or camper you already know it. It's very durable, it's super lightweight, and it's water resistant.

That water resistance was the whole point for me. I wanted packing cubes I could throw my bathing suit and other slightly damp stuff into without soaking everything else, so I really wanted to make sure they covered all of that.

I've only had them for about a month now, but I've been pretty happy with the performance so far. They are what they are. That said, I would not recommend buying them. It's just too expensive for packing cubes, and with how my budgeting has been going lately, I probably could have gone without them.

## A Reality Check on Spending

Which leads me right into the next thing: budgeting.

I've been spending a lot more money recently, and I probably need to do a better job of saving. I'm still saving, and I'm not spending more than I'm making, but I'm also not going to sit here and pretend I couldn't be saving more. At my age, for what I'm making, I really should be.

## Asking for a Raise

And that leads into my next rant, which is that I have a meeting with my boss coming up to talk about salary.

I think I have a strong case for what I want to ask for, but I've never actually been in this position before. I've never asked for a raise or an increase. I've basically just sat back and taken whatever they decided to give me.

But if you want to go anywhere in life, you need to be driven, and you need to be upfront with your employer if you want any kind of meaningful change. Along with that, you have to have the confidence to walk in there and say, this is what I'm worth, and see what you can get.

So I'm excited for the meeting. I'm a little nervous too, but I've been going through basically every scenario he could throw at me, and I feel pretty confident about landing where I'd like to be. If I get there, I'll be a very happy camper for the rest of the year, that's for sure.

## Trips on the Horizon

I think part of what's driving all of this is that I have a lot of trips coming up. I'm going to Barcelona and Portugal, and that's definitely going to cost some money, but it's well worth it.

Whenever I go abroad I want to have a good time and eat well, because you never really know when you'll be back. I count myself very blessed that I can do things like that.
